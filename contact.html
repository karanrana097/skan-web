4. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is there really zero brokerage?

Yes! We charge ₹0 commission to tenants. You connect directly with verified property hosts without middlemen.

Q: How does the Digital Tenant Pass work?

Once your booking is confirmed, your in-app Digital Pass generates a live dynamic QR code for seamless building entry, curfew check-ins, and direct access to room amenities.

Q: Is my booking advance safe?

All token advances are protected in escrow until your scheduled move-in day. You can cancel up to 48 hours beforehand for a 100% full refund.
